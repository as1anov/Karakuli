The geometry of choice.

Geometry carved into the stone of old Azerbaijani walls. Carpets with a rhythm that has no beginning and no end. Shebeke, where a strict lattice turns light into a pattern. This is where Karakuli comes from.

It began as free lines drawn by hand. Page by page they found their order, but kept their liveliness. A diamond is a sign, a line is movement, a circle is the sun with eight paths leading out of it. The sun lights each of them, but does not decide where to go: the choice stays with you.

The pattern is laser-cut from sheet steel, so the lines stay as sharp as in the drawing. The metal is strong and lasting, like the stone of old walls.

A pattern can be drawn, or you can live in it.

I invite you to find your own path in it.
