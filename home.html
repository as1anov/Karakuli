The geometry of choice.

Geometry carved into the stone of old Azerbaijani walls. Carpets with a rhythm that has neither beginning nor end. Shebeke, where a precise lattice transforms light into a pattern. This is where Karakuli comes from.

It began with free-flowing lines drawn by hand. Page by page, they gradually found their order while retaining their liveliness. Let your eyes wander through it like a maze. Some will notice a diamond, others a circle, while some will follow a line and discover something no one else has.

Shop

The pattern is laser-cut from sheet steel, so the lines stay as sharp as in the drawing. The metal is strong and lasting, like the stone of old walls.

A pattern can be drawn, or you can live in it.

I invite you to find your own path in it.
