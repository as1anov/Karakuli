Scroll

It began with old walls.

Geometry carved into the stone of old buildings in Azerbaijan: straight lines, sharp corners, nothing wasted. Our first school of strictness.

Then warm threads and coloured light.

Carpets taught rhythm: diamonds that repeat with no beginning and no end. Shebeke taught that a strict frame can turn light itself into a pattern.

A free hand, put in order.

Karakuli started as free lines drawn by hand. Step by step they found a grid of 90 and 45 degrees, but kept the liveliness of the scribble. A diamond is a sign, a line is a path, a circle is the sun: eight roads lead out of it, and the choice is yours.

From ink on paper to steel: from the light to the lasting.
